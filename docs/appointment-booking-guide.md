# How to Book an Appointment
**Product:** Shree Balaji Multispeciality Hospital Website
**Document Type:** User Guide
**Author:** Bhumika Shah

## 1. Overview

The Appointment Booking feature allows patients to submit appointment requests through the hospital website. Users can provide their personal details, select a department and doctor, and choose their preferred appointment date.

## 2. Prerequisites

Before booking an appointment, make sure you have:

- Access to the hospital website.
- A valid name and email address.
- A contact number.
- Your preferred appointment date.
- The required department and doctor details.

## 3. Steps to Book an Appointment

**Step 1: Open the Website**

Open the Shree Balaji Multispeciality Hospital website in your web browser.

**Step 2: Navigate to the Appointment Section**

Locate and open the appointment booking section.

**Step 3: Enter Personal Details**

Enter your name, email address, and contact number in the respective fields.

**Step 4: Select Appointment Details**

Choose your preferred appointment date, department, and doctor.

**Step 5: Enter Additional Information**

If required, provide additional information in the message field.

**Step 6: Submit the Form**

Review the entered information and click the Submit button.

## 4. Expected Result

After submitting the form, the application should process the appointment request. Verify the actual confirmation or response displayed by the website.

## 5. Troubleshooting

Issue	                                  Suggested Action
Required field is empty	                  Complete all mandatory fields.
Invalid email address	                  Enter a valid email address.
Incorrect contact number	              Check and re-enter the number.
Form submission fails	                  Check your internet connection and try again.

## 6. Conclusion

The appointment booking feature provides a convenient way for patients to submit appointment requests online without visiting the hospital to make the initial request.
